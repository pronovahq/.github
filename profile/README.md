# ProNova

Custom AI agents, MCP servers and workflows for teams with real work to hand off.

ProNova is a Berlin team that builds custom AI agents, custom MCP connectors and hands-on AI training for small and mid-sized businesses, on Claude, OpenAI and Vercel.

**What we build**
- **AI Workflow Lab** (start here): find your first AI builds in 2–3 weeks. You leave with 8–12 specced and priced workflows, a training plan and one working prototype, for €2,500 fixed. The fee counts toward your first build.
- **Custom AI Agents**: an agent that takes one repetitive job off your plate, inside the tools you already use.
- **Custom MCP Builds**: the tools your official connector is missing, built next to it, not instead of it.
- **Team AI Training**: live training on your team's own work, in half-day, full-day or four-week formats.
- **Support plan**: an optional monthly plan that keeps live workflows running as models, APIs and tools change.

Every agent runs alongside your team before it acts on its own.
